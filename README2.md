# PennyScope

> AI-Powered Investment Research Platform for Long-Term Investors

> **Status:** Archived (Paused)

---

## Overview

PennyScope was envisioned as a desktop application that helps investors discover high-quality investment opportunities by combining:

- Fundamental Analysis
- Technical Analysis
- Historical Backtesting
- AI-powered explanations
- Portfolio insights

The long-term goal was to create an investment research platform that allows investors to make better decisions using data instead of emotions.

Unlike traditional stock screeners, PennyScope aimed to explain **why** a stock was attractive rather than simply displaying indicators.

---

## Planned Features

### Market Scanner

- Fundamental metrics

### AI Analysis

Generate human-readable explanations such as:

- Why a stock ranks highly

- Long-term outlook

### Backtesting

Evaluate investment strategies against historical market data.

### Portfolio Analysis

- Risk analysis

## Project Status

This project has been **paused indefinitely**.

During development, it became clear that many of the low-level building blocks required for quantitative investing already exist as mature open-source projects:

- Market data providers
- Technical analysis libraries
- Backtesting frameworks
- Portfolio optimization tools
- Trading engines

Rebuilding these components from scratch would require a significant investment of time while providing limited additional value.

Instead, development efforts are currently focused on products with active users and clearer product-market validation.

---

## Lessons Learned

Working on PennyScope provided valuable experience in:

- Desktop application architecture
- Electron development
- React + TypeScript
- Express APIs
- Prisma ORM
- Financial data modeling
- Investment research workflows

Although the project is paused, these learnings continue to influence future products.

---

## Future Vision

PennyScope may return in a different form.

Instead of rebuilding existing quantitative finance tools, the future direction would focus on:

- AI-powered investment research
- Natural language stock analysis
- Explainable recommendations
- Intelligent portfolio insights
- Workflow automation for investors

The goal would be to leverage existing open-source finance libraries while concentrating development effort on AI and user experience.

---

## Why It Was Paused

Every project competes for time.

At the time of writing, other products demonstrated stronger traction and clearer opportunities for growth.

Rather than dividing focus across multiple large projects, development priorities shifted toward products with active users and immediate impact.

PennyScope remains archived as a reference and may be revisited in the future when the timing and product direction are better aligned.
