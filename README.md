# Stock Intelligence

A desktop-based stock research platform designed for personal use.

The software scans Indian and US markets, collects market data from multiple sources, performs technical, fundamental, sentiment, and risk analysis, and generates stock recommendations for a 1–6 month holding period.

The system is intended to assist investment research, not automate trading.

---

## Core Features

### Market Scanning

* Indian Market Support

  * NSE
  * BSE
  * Screener
  * MoneyControl

* US Market Support

  * Yahoo Finance
  * Finviz
  * MarketWatch

---

### Technical Analysis

Indicators:

* RSI
* MACD
* Moving Averages
* Volume Analysis
* Trend Strength
* Breakout Detection

---

### Fundamental Analysis

Metrics:

* Revenue Growth
* Profit Growth
* Debt to Equity
* Market Capitalization
* Profit Margins
* Valuation Ratios

---

### Sentiment Analysis

Sources:

* News Headlines
* Market News Articles

AI Models:

* Qwen
* DeepSeek
* Llama

Served locally through Ollama.

---

### Risk Analysis

Evaluates:

* Liquidity Risk
* Volatility
* Drawdown Risk
* Debt Risk
* Market Capitalization Risk

---

### Recommendation Engine

Outputs:

* BUY
* WATCH
* AVOID

Includes:

* Confidence Score
* Risk Score
* Explanation
* Supporting Metrics

---

### Backtesting Engine

Allows testing strategies on historical data.

Provides:

* CAGR
* Win Rate
* Maximum Drawdown
* Sharpe Ratio
* Portfolio Performance

Install Dependencies

Generate Prisma Client

npx prisma generate

npx prisma migrate dev
